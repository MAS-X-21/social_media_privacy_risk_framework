Privacy checklist

  • ☑ Profile visibility: Profile is limited to people I know
  • ☑ Personal-information exposure: Profile shows minimal personal details
  • ☑ Contact-information exposure: Email and phone are hidden from non-contacts
  • ☑ Location exposure: Location sharing is off or limited
  • ☑ Workplace / education exposure: Work and school details are limited
  • ☑ Birthday exposure: Birth year is hidden; only day/month shown to friends at most
  • ☐ Family / relationship information: Family links are private
  • ☐ Post visibility: Default post audience is friends or narrower
  • ☑ Friend / follower controls: Friend list is hidden and requests are restricted
  • ☑ Tagging permissions: Tag review is turned on
  • ☑ Third-party application access: Connected apps reviewed in the last 6 months
  • ☑ Account authentication: Strong unique password and secure recovery options
  • ☑ MFA usage: MFA enabled (authenticator app or security key preferred)
  • ☑ Password reuse awareness: Unique passwords stored in a password manager
  • ☑ Login alerts: Login alerts on; active sessions reviewed regularly
  • ☑ Unknown connection requests: Unknown requests are declined / verified first
  • ☑ Suspicious links / messages: Unexpected links are verified before clicking
  • ☑ Photo metadata awareness: Aware of EXIF; location tagging off in camera
  • ☑ Historical posts: Old posts reviewed or limited to a narrower audience
  • ☑ Social-engineering exposure: Requests for money/codes are verified out-of-band

Security-awareness guidance

  • Family / relationship information: Family names and relationships help with 'trusted contact' scams and security-question guessing.
  • Post visibility: Public-by-default posting means every casual post is permanently searchable and screenshot-able.

This is an EDUCATIONAL risk framework based on self-reported answers. It estimates privacy exposure; it is NOT a guarantee that an account will or will not be compromised, and it is not a penetration test or audit.
