Security-awareness guidance

  • Profile visibility: A public profile gives strangers (and automated tools) a ready-made starting point for social engineering.
  • MFA usage: MFA blocks the majority of automated account-takeover attempts. App- or key-based MFA beats SMS.
  • Photo metadata awareness: Photos can embed GPS coordinates and device info if the platform or your upload path does not strip them.
  • Contact-information exposure: Visible contact details enable phishing, SMS scams, SIM-swap targeting and credential-stuffing.
  • Family / relationship information: Family names and relationships help with 'trusted contact' scams and security-question guessing.
