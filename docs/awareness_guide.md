# Security-Awareness Quick Guide

1. **Turn on MFA** (authenticator app or passkey) for social accounts AND the email behind them.
2. **Unique passwords** via a password manager.
3. **Pause on urgency.** Never share one-time codes; verify money requests by voice call.
4. **Audience check** before posting: Friends > Public.
5. **Limit location and routine** details; post after you leave.
6. **Review tags, apps, sessions** monthly or quarterly.
7. **Strip photo metadata** and blur plates, badges, tickets.
8. **Report and block** phishing; tell friends if an account is cloned.
9. **Annual audit** of old posts and profile fields.
